<p>Are you looking for a safe, simple, and reliable calling app that won't bother you with ads or collect your data? 📱✨</p>
<p>You've just found it.</p>

<p><b>Why you'll love this app: ❤️</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>🎨 Customizable colors:</b> Set the colors exactly to your liking.</li>
<li><b>💸 100% free:</b> No fees, no limits—all features are available.</li>
<li><b>🔒 Maximum privacy:</b>
  <ul>
  <li>The app collects no data about you.</li>
  <li>It allows you to save contacts to its own hidden storage, invisible to all other apps.</li>
  </ul>
</li>
<li><b>📡 No internet connection is required:</b> Internet permission is not declared, the application runs offline, nothing is sent or downloaded anywhere!</li>
<li><b>🚫 Ad-free:</b> No pop-ups, no annoyances.</li>
<li><b>🧭 Simple controls:</b> Logical and intuitive—anyone can use it. You can even search for contacts directly via the dialer using T9.</li>
<li><b>👨‍👩‍👧 No more unwanted calls:</b> Check a single box to allow calls only from your saved contacts (blocking spam, scams, and annoying call centers). Perfect for 👶 children and 👵 seniors.</li>
<li><b>💻 Open-source (GNU/GPL v3):</b> You can download the code from my website (<a href="https://mts.speccy.cz/mtsphone.htm">mts.speccy.cz/mtsphone.htm</a>) or <a href="https://github.com/martintobola-cz/czmtsphone">GitHub</a> and review it at any time.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>The app's story 🛠️</b></p>
<p>The original app was created by Slovak developer Tibor Kaputa (Simple Mobile Tools). Unfortunately, he sold it, and the new Israeli company immediately introduced an overpriced subscription model and ads.</p>
<p>Naturally, this led to the creation of a new branch—first the Indian fork "Fossify," and subsequently this Czech 🇨🇿 fork of mine, which gets the app back on the right track and responds quickly to your feedback 💪</p>
